Growden: Plant Care

An iPhone app that tells you when to water each plant in your yard and on your patio, from fruit trees to herbs in pots. It works from your local weather and learns from your soil checks.

Help

Email growden.help@gmail.com with a question, a bug or advice that looks wrong. On a plant's page you can also tap "Does this amount seem wrong?".

Common questions

How do I cancel Growden Plus?
Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Growden. Apple handles billing and refunds, so deleting the app or your account doesn't cancel it.

How do I delete my account?
In Growden, go to Settings, then "Delete account" (or "Delete my data" if you never signed in). This deletes your garden, notes and account from our servers right away. You can also email us to ask.

Why does Growden ask for my ZIP code?
To find the weather where you garden. We keep the ZIP code and the 4 km weather area it falls in, never your GPS location or street address.

I'm not getting reminders.
Growden sends one reminder a day, at the time you picked, and only on days a plant needs water. Heat and frost warnings come when the weather calls for them. If none arrive, check that notifications for Growden are on in the iPhone Settings app.
