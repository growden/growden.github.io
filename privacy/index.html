Growden

Privacy policy

Effective October 5, 2026

The short version: we keep what the app needs to tell you when to water, never your GPS location or street address. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps. You can delete everything from inside the app.

This policy covers the Growden: Plant Care app for iPhone ("Growden", "we"). Questions go to growden.help@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • An account ID. The app creates a random ID the first time you open it, so your garden can sync without a sign-in.
  • Your email address, if you sign in with Apple. Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real one; you choose.
  • Your ZIP code and the 4 km weather area and hardiness zone it falls in, plus your time zone. Never GPS coordinates or a street address.
  • Your garden: your spaces, your plants and how they're planted, waterings, skips, soil checks, notes you write, and your reminder settings.
  • Feedback you send, such as "Does this amount seem wrong?", with what the app showed you at the time.
  • A push notification token, so we can send your reminders.
  • Purchase status for Growden Plus. Apple handles payment; we never see your card.
  • Crash reports: what went wrong in the app, with your device model, iOS version, app version and account ID. Never your notes, photos or location.
  • Usage data, unless you turn it off: which screens you open and what you log (for example "watered" or "soil check: moist"), your plan, your climate region, and your device and app version, under your account ID. Never the text of your notes or your photos.
  • Waitlist email. If you live outside the US and join the waitlist, we keep that email and your country, separate from any account.

Photos

When you use plant ID or a health check, the photo goes through our server to our plant identification provider to get a result. We don't store the photo. A health check result is saved as a note on that plant.

How we use it

To work out when each plant needs water, send your reminders and weather warnings, sync your garden between devices, run Growden Plus, fix crashes, and learn which parts of the app help people. We don't use your data for advertising and we don't sell it.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that run parts of Growden for us. Each gets only what its job needs, and none gets your data for advertising.

  • Our hosting provider stores your account and garden and runs our server.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, payments and notification delivery.
  • A notification service passes reminders to Apple for delivery. It sees your push token and the reminder text, which can include plant names.
  • A subscription service keeps track of your Growden Plus subscription, under your account ID.
  • A plant identification service receives the photos you send for plant ID or a health check.
  • A crash reporting service receives crash reports.
  • An analytics service receives usage data, if you leave it on.

Email us if you want to know which companies they are. Weather comes from public weather services. We ask them about weather areas, never about you. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Usage data: in Settings, tap Privacy and turn off "Share usage data".
  • Notifications: turn them off in the iPhone Settings app.
  • Delete everything: in Settings, tap "Delete account" (or "Delete my data" if you never signed in). This deletes your account and garden from our servers right away, deletes your usage data at our analytics service and your record at our subscription service, and, if you signed in with Apple, disconnects Growden from your Apple ID. It doesn't cancel a Growden Plus subscription; do that in the iPhone Settings app.
  • A copy, a correction or anything else: email us and we'll answer within 30 days.

How long we keep it

Your account and garden stay until you delete them. Crash reports are deleted after 30 days. Usage data is kept for up to a year. Waitlist emails are kept until we launch in your country or you ask us to remove them.

Where it's stored

Our providers store data in the United States. We protect it with encryption in transit and access rules that let each account read only its own garden.

Children

Growden isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For changes that affect how we use your data, we'll tell you in the app first.
