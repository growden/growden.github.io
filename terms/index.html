Watering advice is an estimate

Growden works out when and roughly how much to water from weather data, typical values for each kind of plant, and what you tell us. It can't see your soil, your sprinklers or your plant's health, and weather forecasts are sometimes wrong. Check the soil before you water, and use your own judgment, especially for valuable trees and plants under stress. We aren't responsible for plants lost or damaged by following, or not following, the app's advice.

Plant ID and health checks can be wrong

Results from a photo are a best guess. Never use them to decide whether a plant is safe to eat, touch or keep around children or pets.
